How AlertIndia Works
Step 01
🛰️
Satellite + Sensor Data
We pull river levels, temperature, rainfall and air quality data from ISRO, ESA, and CPCB every 30 minutes.
Step 02
🤖
AI Risk Detection
Our model detects dangerous patterns hours before they reach critical levels — across all hazard types simultaneously.
Step 03
📡
First to Alert
WhatsApp & SMS alerts go directly to panchayat heads, ASHA workers and community leaders — skipping slow government chains.
Step 04
🏘️
Any Location
Search any village, town or city in India. Or use your current location. Coverage down to local neighbourhood level.
📡
Sentinel Mode BETA
Activates real-time acoustic + vibration monitoring via your device sensors. Detects unusual patterns — distant thunder, ground tremors, industrial alerts.
